# SUPPLEMENT — §6-octies · URBAN BEATS (generic city content, campaign-agnostic)

*Drop-in setting-flavor layer for the v5 master prompts. Campaign-agnostic: no proper nouns, no
named factions, no metaplot. When the party is in any city, this replaces the generic §6 flat-d100
result with an urban-flavored situation. It is the **ambient** source for urban play. It does NOT
carry a specific campaign's cast or threads: a chartered module's own d100 tables (lifted from the
published adventure, or crafted for its charter) live in that campaign's module material, and its
quest-linked beats come from the §6-septies live content table, not here.*

---

## HOW TO USE

Roll the §6 content chain as normal, as world dice through the engine in one call: **nature d6 →
content d100 → band → urban d20** (this table replaces the band's environment cast and generic
intersection when the setting is a city). Then read the urban entry as a **situation with an
implication** and skin it to the local fiction. Every entry is deliberately faction-neutral and
name-free; the DM attaches the city's own people and places at the table. The bands are the §6
interleaved bands (30 / 30 / 30 / 10, read by the content d100's last digit), so the d100 lands in a
band and you roll that band's d20 below.

- **Last digit 1, 4, 7 → Confrontation d20** (nonviolent-confrontational)
- **Last digit 2, 5, 8 → Aid d20** (nonviolent-protective)
- **Last digit 3, 6, 9 → Fight d20** (a fight surfaces)
- **Last digit 0 → City wildcard d20**

Reserve the last slot of each band for the strange or the reframing. A result is a roll, and it
stands (master prompt §1-sexies rule 4): no reroll, combining, or overruling except by a RAW or
player-invoked mechanic.

## 01-25 · CONFRONTATION (d20) — social or situational pressure, no blades drawn

| d20 | Situation |
|---|---|
| 1 | A watch patrol stops the party for routine questioning, but one officer clearly recognizes someone. |
| 2 | A courier presses a sealed letter into a PC's hand, addressed in a handwriting no one knows. |
| 3 | An operative of a local power clocks the party and goes very still, waiting, saying nothing. |
| 4 | A cloaked figure falls into step beside the party and speaks quietly without looking: a message from someone. |
| 5 | A noble's steward delivers a formal card: a house requests a meeting today, if convenient. |
| 6 | An official with a ledger is writing something down outside the party's lodging, and has not knocked. |
| 7 | The party rounds a corner into a standoff between two groups they recognize; both turn to look at them. |
| 8 | A runner tracks the party down: a contact needs them specifically, and says it is important. |
| 9 | A shop the party frequents refuses them entry; through the window the owner makes a small apologetic gesture. |
| 10 | A uniformed official the party has seen before says a single name, then walks away. |
| 11 | A stranger walks a half-block beside the party, naming a place they have been, and asks to discuss it. |
| 12 | Someone calls out the name of the party's establishment or trade in a too-familiar way, in public. |
| 13 | Fresh chalk on a wall the party passes is a recognizable likeness of one of them. |
| 14 | A laborer stops mid-work and watches the party pass, not approaching, not looking away. |
| 15 | A posted notice concerns a property or permit that matches something the party holds. |
| 16 | A very polite buyer offers a very reasonable price for something the party carries, naming it exactly. |
| 17 | A stranger insists a PC is someone else, someone they are owed money by, apologizes, looks shaken. |
| 18 | A temple or order posts a notice requesting a meeting with "the party," signed and addressed directly. |
| 19 | A mark the party left (a sign, a favor, a debt) has been altered by a second, unknown hand. |
| 20 | A carriage with an unfamiliar sigil blocks the route; a gloved hand, no face, extends a sealed envelope. |

## 26-50 · AID (d20) — someone or something needs help; engagement, not violence

| d20 | Situation |
|---|---|
| 1 | A cart has shed a wheel and blocked the road; the owner is small, the growing crowd is not. |
| 2 | A person sits in the street, unwell and unmoving, while passersby give them a wide berth. |
| 3 | A child follows the party at a distance, with clear intent, too afraid to approach. |
| 4 | Someone argues loudly with an official outside a building; witnesses stay silent. |
| 5 | A cornered street animal is in genuine distress, and children are crowding, not helping. |
| 6 | An acquaintance turns up somewhere they should not be, holding something they "found." |
| 7 | An apprentice is literally stuck in a training device outside a shop while the master laughs inside. |
| 8 | A familiar face is off their usual route, moving fast, scared, and stops dead on seeing the party. |
| 9 | A balked animal blocks the only gate to a courtyard where someone is audibly trying to get out. |
| 10 | A broadsheet runner is selling, on this corner, a story that could cause the party real trouble. |
| 11 | An old vendor is being pushed from a stall they clearly held yesterday, by someone with paperwork. |
| 12 | A calm shopkeeper stands watching a shut door across the street, and looks at the party as they pass. |
| 13 | An animal is left tied at the party's doorstep with a note: "couldn't keep it." |
| 14 | A delivery of good food arrives at the party's lodging, paid for, with no sender named. |
| 15 | A contact sends word: something surfaced in their work, not urgent, come when you can. |
| 16 | A festival or market has made a key route impassable; go through the crowd or go around. |
| 17 | Someone works on an injured person in an alley and looks up: "I need help moving him." |
| 18 | An object the party carries reacts, faintly and physically, as they pass a specific building. |
| 19 | A child arrives asking for a specific member of the party's household, by name, and won't say how they know. |
| 20 | A helper the party once aided reappears, worse off, carrying one piece of useful, dangerous news. |

## 51-90 · FIGHT (d20) — combat surfaces; resolve through the combat suite (§4)

| d20 | Situation |
|---|---|
| 1 | A street crew waits at an intersection the party uses; they have a description of someone. |
| 2 | Officers intercept the party on a specific, inconvenient charge and want them to come now. |
| 3 | An enforcer checking a location sees the party where they are not supposed to be. |
| 4 | Trackers have followed a trail (an object, a rumor, a debt) from elsewhere straight to the party. |
| 5 | Hired professionals corner the party in a narrow passage with one instruction: bring someone in. |
| 6 | A gang the party has not met is robbing a shop they pass; a lookout stands outside. |
| 7 | A household's guard spots the party in public and breaks for backup before engaging. |
| 8 | An unaligned crew has been following the party at a plausible distance for two blocks. |
| 9 | Attackers converge from doorways, marks hidden, not here to talk. |
| 10 | A protection racket makes its pitch to the party on a market street, not knowing who they are. |
| 11 | Shooters open from cover across a courtyard, no warning and no demand. |
| 12 | A checkpoint on the route is too official to ignore, and someone is checking for a specific thing. |
| 13 | A fire draws a crowd, and in it, someone moving something illicit recognizes the party. |
| 14 | A carriage runs the party's street at speed; at their position it slows and a crossbow comes out. |
| 15 | A lone skilled fighter steps into the party's path at a chokepoint and does not move. |
| 16 | A hired brawler is waiting at the party's door, unhurried, and names one of them. |
| 17 | A mob moves through the district toward a site the party cares about, carrying torches. |
| 18 | A watched rooftop reveals two spotters who have logged the party's movements all morning. |
| 19 | The party arrives to find a friend's door forced, a guard down but breathing, and voices inside. |
| 20 | A well-drilled squad sets a cordon around the block the party is in; a notice is being nailed up. |

## 91-00 · CITY WILDCARD (d20) — off-pattern; let the dice genuinely surprise

| d20 | Situation |
|---|---|
| 1 | Something in the party's lodging has been rearranged overnight, deliberately, and no one can say why. |
| 2 | A member of the household has left, taking nothing but leaving one pointed object behind. |
| 3 | An object the party keeps has changed temperature or state overnight, with no cause visible. |
| 4 | A figure from the party's dreams or past is on the same street, doing something banal, not yet seeing them. |
| 5 | An anonymous note names a private truth about a PC, with no demand attached, just the sentence. |
| 6 | An item is simply gone from a sealed container that shows no sign of entry. |
| 7 | A street performer's show is a thinly veiled retelling of something the party actually did. |
| 8 | A hidden object surfaces behind a loose board: old, deliberately concealed, not lost. |
| 9 | A mark or shrine the party knows has been cleaned or altered overnight by a careful, unknown hand. |
| 10 | A city monument or fixture behaves wrongly for a moment, briefly, and nobody can explain it. |
| 11 | A deadline the party is under changes without warning, and a messenger makes sure they know. |
| 12 | Two strangers approach separately within the hour, each certain the party is someone they are not. |
| 13 | An animal follows the party across the whole district and will not be shaken. |
| 14 | A rumor about the party, wildly wrong and weirdly specific, is already spreading ahead of them. |
| 15 | A door the party knows was locked stands open, with nothing obviously taken. |
| 16 | The weather turns strange over one street only, and the locals have already stopped noticing. |
| 17 | A stranger returns a lost thing the party did not know they had lost, and asks nothing. |
| 18 | A coincidence stacks too neatly: three unrelated things the party touched all move on the same day. |
| 19 | Someone the party wronged and forgot sends a gift, gracious and unsettling. |
| 20 | The city itself seems to pause on the party for a heartbeat, an ordinary day history will later mark. |

## ONE-LINE SUMMARY

> In a city, roll content d100 → band → the band's urban d20 here. Skin the faction-neutral
> situation to the local fiction, surface it, and yield. Campaign-specific city tables and
> quest-linked beats come from elsewhere (the charter's module material and §6-septies); this is
> the reusable ambient-urban layer.
